STRATEGY6 Task 6 audit
======================

Transcript slice reviewed
-------------------------
- Move 46 onward, once Black is clearly better after the queen trade.
- Key conversion points:
  - `...Kh8`
  - `...Nd6`
  - `...b5`
  - `...Nxe4`
  - `...Rxa4`
  - the later king capture / cleanup phase

Findings
--------
1. The transcript conversion already had a strong structural edge, but it wasted time with small king/piece detours before cashing the queenside pawn mass and simplifying further.
2. The cleaner practical route was to keep choosing forcing conversion moves: redeploy the knight toward the queenside files, keep the b-pawn rolling, and take loose pawns / exchanges when they shorten the game.
3. Existing conversion coverage from earlier phases already handled several generic Task 6 bullets:
   - `tests/test_ai_quality.py` covers queen-trade simplification and king improvement in won endings.
   - `tests/test_ai_search.py` already rejects harmless checking when a clean simplification exists.
4. The missing STRATEGY6-specific gap was root-choice quality in winning conversions. Quiet ordering already knew about conversion plans, but root tie-breaks did not yet reuse that guidance, so deeper transcript positions could still choose less forcing winning moves.

Task 6 implementation direction
-------------------------------
- Added `winning_conversion_root_bonus()` in `chess_game/chess/conversion_guidance.py` and fed it into `ai_search_helpers.py` so root choices in winning positions inherit the same conversion geometry already used by quiet ordering.
- Added transcript-backed regressions for:
  - preferring `...Nd6` as the clean knight redeployment,
  - preferring `...Rxa4` as the clean queenside cash-in,
  - preserving depth-3 queen-trade simplification in a clearly won ending.

# Rust Zobrist Hashing and Repetition Identity

This document defines the repetition-identity contract implemented by `chess-core`.

## Versioned deterministic schedule

`Position::ZOBRIST_VERSION` is `1`. The version covers the seed, key-generation algorithm, table layout, and canonical repetition policy. Any change to those inputs must increment the version. Hashes from different versions are not interchangeable.

Version 1 uses compile-time deterministic keys generated by a SplitMix64-style mixer from the fixed seed `0x9d39_247e_3377_6d41`. The key schedule is:

1. `2 × 6 × 64` piece-square keys, indexed by color, piece kind, and square;
2. one side-to-move key;
3. sixteen complete castling-state keys, indexed by the four castling-right bits;
4. eight en-passant-file keys.

The tables are deterministic across builds and platforms. They are internal engine data, not a stable persistence or network ABI.

## Canonical repetition identity

The hash includes:

- every piece, its color, kind, and square;
- the side to move;
- the complete castling-right state;
- the en-passant file only when the side to move has at least one legal en-passant capture.

The hash excludes:

- the halfmove clock;
- the fullmove number;
- move history that does not change the current legal position identity;
- FEN text formatting.

This matches chess repetition semantics: positions that differ only in counters are identical, while castling rights and a legally actionable en-passant target distinguish positions.

## En-passant canonicalization

A FEN en-passant target is hashed only when all of the following hold:

1. the target square is empty;
2. the pawn that would be captured exists on the correct square;
3. an adjacent pawn of the side to move can geometrically capture the target;
4. after removing the moving pawn and captured pawn and placing the moving pawn on the target, that side's king is not attacked.

The legality test is occupancy-based and non-recursive. It directly evaluates pawn, knight, king, bishop/queen, and rook/queen attacks against the post-capture king position. A merely present but unusable FEN target contributes no key.

## Authoritative and incremental paths

`Position::recomputed_zobrist()` is the authoritative full-state calculation. Position construction and FEN parsing derive the stored key from authoritative state; callers cannot inject an arbitrary hash.

Legal move application updates the stored key by XOR deltas for:

- the old and new canonical en-passant state;
- the old and new castling state;
- side to move;
- moving piece source and destination;
- captures, including en passant;
- castling rook movement;
- promotion pawn removal and promoted-piece placement.

Undo tokens retain the exact previous key, so unmake restores it in constant time. Debug builds compare stored and fully recomputed keys after make and unmake.

## Verification contract

Tests cover:

- fixed versioned hash fixtures;
- counter exclusion;
- legal, non-capturable, and king-unsafe en-passant targets;
- quiet moves, double pushes, captures, en passant, both castles, promotions, promotion captures, and castling-right-changing rook captures;
- deterministic randomized legal sequences where the incremental key must equal full recomputation after every ply;
- complete reverse restoration to the original position and key.

Task 10 may use this key as the position identity for repetition history. Collision handling remains a game/search-layer policy; this task defines the canonical 64-bit identity input.
